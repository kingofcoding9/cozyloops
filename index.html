Meet Kayce

Hi, I'm Kayce! My journey with yarn began years ago on a cold winter evening. What started as a hobby to make gifts for friends and family quickly spiraled into a deep-rooted passion for textile arts and design.

At Cozy Loops, I believe that clothing and accessories should do more than just look good—they should make you feel comforted, confident, and warm. That's why I hand-select every skein of yarn, prioritizing softness, durability, and sustainable practices wherever possible.

Whether you're picking up a chunky beanie for the slopes or a delicate crochet top for the summer festival season, know that hours of careful planning, counting stitches, and genuine love went into creating your piece.

Thank you for supporting slow fashion and handmade art.
